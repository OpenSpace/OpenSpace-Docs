## Overview

The Nearby Stars is, more formally, the Gaia Catalog of Nearby Stars (GCNS). Gaia is a revolutionary mission to get precise information on the positions and motions of billions of stars. See the index page on [Stars](content--milky-way--stars--gaia) for more information on the Gaia mission and data release.


## Litations of the Nearby Stars

The Nearby Stars maps every star within 100 parsecs, or 326 light years. This is a *limited* sample of stars, but is a complete catalog within these limitations.

### Cool stars limitation

The coolest stars grow too faint to see beyond 100 parsecs, so the first limitation is the distance cutoff so that we can have a complete sample that includes the cooler, dimmer stars.
